# Future Ideas

This document is a collection of ideas for future versions of The Foreman.

Ideas listed here are **not commitments**. They represent possibilities, experiments, and long-term concepts that may be explored as HardHead Works grows.

The purpose of this document is simple:

**Never lose a good idea.**

# The Workshop Operating System

The long-term vision is for The Foreman to become the operating system for HardHead Works.

Instead of managing only one part of the business, it should coordinate the workshop as a whole.

Projects.

Inventory.

Budget.

Production.

Maintenance.

Scheduling.

Automation.

Artificial intelligence.

Everything working together through one modular system.

---

# Guiding Rule

No idea is forgotten.

Ideas remain here until one of three things happens:

1. It becomes part of the official roadmap.

2. It is intentionally discarded.

3. It inspires something even better.

Innovation begins by writing ideas down before they are needed.
